# Music workflow for Arkmon

The user asked to retain the audio lessons from the Arkanmeisterin theme. The current arrangement is a sampled orchestral sketch with a basic stereo mix. Treat peak normalization, a clean loop seam and Unity playback tests as technical checks.

1. Keep the motif recognizable. Work from separate stems and vary phrase entrances, note lengths and swells. For the Arkanmeisterin, use fewer overlapping sampled horns and voices. Let an exposed line of recorded strings or winds carry the rise, then bring the brass in for the title.
2. Audition the mix in the scene. Listen from the carriage door through the title and the conversation with hoofbeats, footsteps and the reveal cue enabled. Leave room for the title sound and for the softer dialogue section. Compare the old and new cue at matched loudness so a volume increase cannot decide the comparison.
3. Master after the performance and scene mix are approved. Check headroom, stereo balance and the seam over several repeats. Confirm the result in Unity and share a recording of that context.
